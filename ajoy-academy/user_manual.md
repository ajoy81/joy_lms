# 🎓 Ajoy Academy - User Manual

Welcome to Ajoy Academy! This is a comprehensive guide to navigating and utilizing the Learning Management System (LMS) web application. Ajoy Academy is designed for three primary roles: **Teachers**, **Parents**, and **Children (Students)**.

---

## 🔑 1. Getting Started: Registration & Login

When you first access the app, you will be greeted by the Login/Register screen.
- **Register:** Create a new account by providing a username, password, full name, and selecting your Role (Teacher, Parent, or Child). You can also select a fun avatar emoji!
- **Login:** Enter your username and password to securely log into your designated dashboard.

---

## 👨‍🏫 2. Teacher Dashboard

The Teacher Dashboard is the command center for course creation and student management.

### Linking Students
- Use the **Link a Student** section on the main page to enter a child's username and connect them to your account. This allows you to track their progress and assign them courses.

### Course Builder
- Navigate to **Course Management** in the sidebar.
- Click **+ Create New Course** to define course metadata (Title, Category, Difficulty, Age Group, Estimated Hours, Thumbnail).
- **The Course Flowchart:** The left sidebar displays a nested flowchart tree. Click **+ Add Module** to organize your course into sections, and **+ Add Lesson** within those modules to add content.
- **Lesson Types:** You can add YouTube Videos, Rich Text notes, PDF file uploads, External Links, and Quizzes.
- **Editing & Reordering:** Use the inline tertiary buttons (`✏️`, `▲`, `▼`, `🗑️`) to effortlessly edit, reorder, and delete modules and lessons.
- **Publishing:** Once ready, toggle the "Published" switch in the course editor so students can see it!

### Student Preview
- Switch to the **Student Preview** tab in the Course Builder to see a 100% pixel-perfect replica of what your students will experience, complete with mock progress bars, estimated timing tags, and the exact visual layout.

---

## 👪 3. Parent Dashboard

The Parent Dashboard is designed for tracking and managing your children's educational journey.

### Linking Children
- Similar to Teachers, use the **Link a Child** section on the main page to connect your children's accounts using their usernames.

### Assigning Courses
- Navigate to the **📚 Assign Course** tab.
- Select your child and browse all published courses.
- Choose whether the course is **Suggested** or **Mandatory**, and click "Assign to Child".

### Course Management & Preview
- Parents also have access to the **Course Builder** to create custom, personalized micro-courses for their children.
- Use the **Student Preview** to verify the content exactly as your child will see it before assigning it.

### Analytics & Activity
- Click on **📊 View Progress** under any child's profile to see deep analytics on their performance, including total points, active streaks, quiz scores, and detailed timeline logs of exactly what lessons they watched and when.

---

## 👧 4. Child (Student) Dashboard

The Student Dashboard is a gamified, distraction-free learning environment.

### Enrolled Courses
- The home screen displays a clean grid of all courses assigned to the student. 
- Courses marked **Mandatory** feature a red alert badge to prioritize learning.

### The Course Player (Learning Mode)
- When a student opens a course, they enter the learning interface.
- **Course Flow Sidebar:** The left panel provides a beautiful, indented flowchart of Modules and Lessons. 
- **Global Progress Bar:** A vibrant green progress bar sits at the top, tracking exact completion percentage based on lesson duration.
- **Active Lesson Tracking:** The currently playing lesson is highlighted with a green tick (`✅`).

### Completing Lessons & Earning Points
- For videos, the system automatically tracks watch time (`5.0m`) and completion percentage (`100.0%`).
- Once a lesson is finished, students click **Mark Complete ✅** to earn reward points.
- Students can also mark previously completed lessons as **Revise** to earn fractional revision points and boost their retention metrics.

### Gamification & Leaderboard
- Students earn points for every action (completing lessons, passing quizzes, doing homework).
- The **Leaderboard** shows top performers across the platform, encouraging healthy competition!

## ⚙️ 5. Global UI Features

- **Dark Mode / Light Mode:** Toggle between themes using the `🌓 Theme` switch in the sidebar.
- **Mobile Mode:** Switch to `📱💻 View Mode: Mobile` to constrain the app width for a phone-optimized layout.
- **Dictionary Search:** Access the integrated 'Xobdo' dictionary directly from the sidebar for quick definitions during learning.
